# Pre-call brief: Wayfair

## 1. Deal snapshot
- Stage: Renewal - 75 days out; Product interest: Enterprise + SSO/SCIM [1]
- ARR: $210,000 USD; Renewal date: 2026-12-12 [1]
- Owner: Jamie Reyes; Champion: VP Information Security (name not on file) [1]
- Last activity: 2026-09-14, Executive sponsor meeting [1]
- Rep notes: Largest account in territory; exec sponsor very engaged and asking about AI-agent credential management roadmap [1]

## 2. Company context
- HQ: Boston, MA; Employees: 10,000+; Revenue: $5B+; Ownership: Public [2]
- Identity provider: Okta; Tech stack: GCP, Kubernetes, Salesforce [2]

## 3. Recent signals
1. Q2 2026 results: strongest free cash flow since 2020, adjusted EBITDA $242M vs. $230M expected, active customers 21.7M (Aug 4, 2026) [3][4]
2. Q1 2026 results: 7% revenue growth, 5.2% adjusted EBITDA margin (strongest Q1 in 5 years), management targeting 20%+ mid-term organic growth [5]
3. Wayfair to close Erlanger, KY warehouse (~215 jobs), closure effective on or around Sept 30, 2026, phased layoffs through 2026 [6]
4. Former Wayfair Global Head of Cybersecurity/CISO Marnie Wilking has since moved to Booking.com as CISO; her Wayfair role predates the current champion listed in CRM [7][8]
5. No relevant recent news found on Wayfair-specific data breaches or credential incidents.

## 4. Risks and open questions
- Champion is identified only by title ("VP Information Security"), no name on file — confirm current champion identity given known past CISO turnover (Marnie Wilking left for Booking.com) [1][7][8]. Inference: leadership may have changed again since; worth verifying before the call.
- Warehouse layoffs and past restructuring rounds could signal cost scrutiny on new spend, though these are on the operations/logistics side, not IT security [6]. Inference: unclear if this affects security budget.
- Financials show growth and improving margins, which may support renewal/expansion case [3][4][5].
- Identity provider is Okta [2] — confirm how this integrates with our SSO/SCIM offering ahead of the call.

## 5. Talking points
1. Congratulate on strong Q2 2026 results — strongest free cash flow since 2020 [3].
2. Tie AI-agent credential management roadmap (per rep notes) to Wayfair's continued growth and tech modernization efforts [1][5].
3. Confirm current champion's name/title, referencing known past leadership change at Wayfair (Wilking → Booking.com) [7][8]. Inference: verify if current champion is new to role.
4. Discuss SSO/SCIM integration with existing Okta deployment [2][1].
5. Inference: Given company-wide restructuring/cost discipline (Erlanger closure), consider framing renewal around efficiency/ROI rather than expansion [6].
