## 2. Company context
- HQ: Long Island City, NY; Employees: 10,000+ (CRM lists 23,000) [1][2]
- Revenue band: $5B+; Ownership: Public [2]
- Identity provider: Microsoft Entra ID; Tech stack: Azure, SAP, ServiceNow [2]

## 3. Recent signals
1. JetBlue CISO Keith Anderson departed for Travelers; he had held the "VP, Technology Infrastructure & Chief Information Security Officer" title at JetBlue [3]
2. Q2 2026 results: EPS of -$0.66, beating estimates of -$0.70 [4]
3. Q1 2026: net loss of $319M, wider than the prior year's $208M loss; adjusted EPS missed estimates [5]
4. JetBlue is closing its Newark and LaGuardia operational bases; company says no layoffs, affected staff can bid/transfer to other bases [6][7]
5. JetForward turnaround plan targets $310M incremental EBIT in 2026, building to $850–950M by 2027, per Q4 2025 results [8]

## 4. Risks and open questions
- Champion status is unclear/conflicting in CRM notes — must confirm who the current Director of IT Security is before the call [1]
- Inference: The confirmed departure of CISO Keith Anderson [3] is a different title than the CRM's listed champion ("Director of IT Security"), so it cannot be assumed to be the same person — but it does suggest security-leadership turnover at JetBlue worth probing.
- Financial pressure: widening losses in Q1 2026 and only a narrow beat in Q2 2026 [4][5] could affect budget appetite at renewal time.
- Operational disruption (base closures, route cuts per unverified social posts) may be consuming IT/security bandwidth; only the Newark/LaGuardia closures are confirmed by stronger sources [6][7].
- CRM employee count (23,000) [1] conflicts with firmographic band (10,000+) [2] — likely just a banding difference, but worth noting.

## 5. Talking points
1. Open by confirming the current owner of the IT Security function, given the conflicting CRM notes [1].
2. Acknowledge JetBlue's cost-discipline push (JetForward plan) [8] and frame the renewal around efficiency/ROI, not added spend.
3. Note recent CISO turnover [3] as a reason to re-validate security priorities and stakeholder buy-in ahead of renewal.
4. Inference: With Microsoft Entra ID as the identity provider [2], emphasize integration/compatibility to reduce switching friction.
5. Use the 50-days-out renewal timeline [1] to propose locking a decision meeting before month-end budget cycles tighten further.
