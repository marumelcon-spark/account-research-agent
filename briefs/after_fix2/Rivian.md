## 3. Recent signals
1. Rivian raised FY2026 delivery guidance to 65,000–70,000 vehicles and narrowed adjusted EBITDA loss guidance to $1.8B–$2.0B in Q2 2026 earnings (Jul 30, 2026) [3].
2. Rivian secured a conditional $4.5B DOE loan for its Georgia plant expansion, with draws expected in early 2027; reported a $506M non-cash gain from Mind Robotics deconsolidation (Q1 2026 earnings) [4].
3. Rivian laid off several hundred employees (under 2% of workforce), mostly in service/customer teams, one week after R2 SUV deliveries began (Jun 16, 2026) [5][6].
4. This follows ~600 layoffs (about 4.5% of workforce) in October 2025 [6].
5. Rivian lost $3.6B last year and has not yet turned an annual profit, per CNBC coverage of the June 2026 layoffs [5].

No security-incident or CISO-specific news found relevant to this account.

## 4. Risks and open questions
- Major CRM gaps: no champion identified, no last activity logged, no rep notes on file — deal appears to have little to no documented engagement yet [1].
- Company has had two rounds of layoffs in under a year (Oct 2025 and Jun 2026), which could affect budget appetite or buying-center stability [5][6].
- Ongoing large losses ($3.6B last year) despite improving guidance — worth probing whether security/IT spend is under scrutiny [4][5].

- Inference: With qualification-stage status and a Feb 2027 target date, this is early-stage; call likely needs to focus on discovery rather than late-stage deal mechanics.

2. Given recent layoffs in service/customer teams, explore whether security/IT headcount reductions increase reliance on automated password-security tooling [5][6].
