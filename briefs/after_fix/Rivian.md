## 4. Risks and open questions
- No champion, last activity, or rep notes on file — deal appears to have little to no documented engagement yet. **Open question: confirm current relationship status.**
- No ARR recorded, consistent with "New business - Qualification" stage — deal is early-stage. [1]
- Recent layoffs (customer/service teams) and cost-cutting pressure could affect budget appetite or buying timeline. [3][4]
- Company is absorbing a $250M litigation settlement and heavy losses ($1.8-2.1B projected for 2026) — Inference: this may tighten security tooling budgets or, conversely, increase appetite for risk-reduction if tied to compliance/IPO scrutiny. [7][8]
- Employee count discrepancy: CRM shows 15,000 vs. firmographic "10,000+" band — not necessarily contradictory, but worth confirming current headcount post-layoffs. [1][2]

## 5. Talking points
1. Rivian uses Microsoft Entra ID for identity — worth confirming if MFA/passwordless is already deployed or a current gap. [2]
2. Given ongoing cost discipline (layoffs, delayed profitability target), position password security as a way to reduce breach/helpdesk costs rather than a big new spend. [3][5] Inference: framing.
3. With a public company facing fresh litigation exposure ($250M IPO settlement), strengthening security posture and reducing credential-related risk could resonate with risk/compliance stakeholders. [7]
4. No champion identified yet — first call should focus on identifying the right economic buyer/security stakeholder given the qualification stage. [1] Inference.
5. AWS/SAP stack suggests enterprise-scale infrastructure; ask about current SSO/credential management practices across these systems. [2]
